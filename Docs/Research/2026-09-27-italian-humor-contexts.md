# Italian corpus: cultural and linguistic authoring brief

Research date: 27 September 2026. New texts are original AI-authored Italian, not harvested jokes, translations of another collection, or native-reviewed writing. The collection describes settings in Italy; it does not equate Italian language with citizenship or claim that all Italians share a comic personality. Existing `it.json` remains unchanged.

## Language and comic approach

Regional Italian and the distinct local languages commonly called dialects cannot be reduced to administrative borders or treated as deficient standard Italian. The Crusca describes regional vocabulary and overlapping linguistic/social variation. New jokes therefore use broadly readable Italian, retain local food and place names when meaningful, and do not manufacture dialect speech or mock accents. Regional terms such as Trieste’s coffee vocabulary provide situated misunderstandings without making the speaker a stereotype. Sources: [Crusca: regionalismi e geosinonimi](https://accademiadellacrusca.it/it/consulenza/regionalismi-e-geosinonimi/310), [Crusca: standard and regional Italian](https://id.accademiadellacrusca.org/articoli/italiano-standard-e-italiano-regionale-uso-corretto-vs-uso-errato-il-caso-del-siciliano-piedi-piedi/26063), [Crusca: ironismo](https://accademiadellacrusca.it/it/consulenza/ironismo-va-bene-autoironismo-un-po-meno/24273).

Wordplay uses Italian meanings, sound, grammatical expectations and idioms rather than English puns with Italian nouns. Household negotiations, affectionate misunderstandings, exaggerated procedures and contrasts between ceremony and practical needs give the nine categories different angles. Institutional descriptions of traditions inform settings, never borrowed punchlines or imitation of named performers.

## Concrete regional contexts

**Naples and Sicily.** UNESCO describes pizzaiuolo practice as embodied skill involving dough, rotation, oven work, transmission and social participation. Use the actual craft and its practical rhythms, not an assertion that every Italian loves pizza. Sicilian Opera dei Pupi includes distinct Palermo and Catania traditions, craftsmanship, improvised dialogue and a changing relationship with audiences; new puppet scenes are original, not adaptations of repertoire. Sicily also provides granita with a brioche’s *tuppo*, ricotta-filled cannoli, martorana fruit and Modica chocolate. Sources: [UNESCO pizzaiuolo](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/art-of-neapolitan-pizzaiuolo-00722), [Italian UNESCO commission](https://www.unesco.it/it/iniziative-dellunesco/patrimonio-culturale-immateriale/larte-del-pizzaiuolo-napoletano/), [UNESCO Opera dei Pupi](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/opera-dei-pupi-sicilian-puppet-theatre-00011), [Italia.it: Sicilian foods](https://www.italia.it/it/sicilia/cosa-fare/prodotti-piatti-tipici-siciliani).

**Bologna, Liguria, Puglia.** Bologna’s porticoes are everyday covered public passages, not simply a landmark backdrop. Genoese pesto supplies the tactile work of mortar, basil and pestle; focaccia supplies a specific local bakery scene. Puglian orecchiette and cime di rapa provide shape and preparation; trulli provide a different architectural setting from northern cities. These scenes should generate different jokes rather than substitute place names. Sources: [Bologna Welcome: porticoes](https://www.bolognawelcome.com/it/informazioni/home-portici-unesco), [Visit Genoa: city guide and pesto](https://www.visitgenoa.it/sites/default/files/2025-06/Nel%20cuore%20di%20Genova%20ITA_OL.pdf), [Visit Genoa: focaccia](https://www.visitgenoa.it/it/focaccia-genovese), [Italia.it: Puglian flavors](https://www.italia.it/it/puglia/cosa-fare/la-puglia-dei-sapori-made-in-italy), [Italia.it: Puglia](https://www.italia.it/it/puglia).

**Veneto, Piedmont, Friuli Venezia Giulia, Lombardy.** Venetian masks supply playful identity and practical costume scenes; Ivrea’s orange battle is a particular organized carnival practice, not an invitation to throw food at strangers. Trieste’s *capo in b* is a local coffee order served in a glass, which supports precise lexical humor. Cremona’s violin-making is a learned craft with apprenticeship and formal education; jokes can use tuning, patience, wood and beginner practice without ridiculing artisans. Sources: [Venice Carnival official masks](https://carnevale.venezia.it/en/the-venice-carnival/masks-and-costumes/), [Italia.it: Ivrea](https://www.italia.it/it/piemonte/cosa-fare/carnevale-di-ivrea), [PromoTurismoFVG: Trieste cafés](https://www.turismofvg.it/it/arte-e-cultura/itinerari-tematici/i-caffe-di-trieste?LangSetCMS=it), [Ministry of Culture: Cremona craft](https://unesco.cultura.gov.it/en/projects/traditional-violin-craftsmanship-in-cremona/).

**Shared but non-universal customs.** The Befana’s stocking and broom, village sagre, Ferragosto outings and contemporary rail/office routines allow varied family, child and workplace situations. Customs vary, and not everyone participates. Rail or administrative incidents are fictional, not guidance about live services or rules. Sources: [Italia.it: Befana](https://www.italia.it/it/italia/cosa-fare/storia-ed-eventi-per-la-befana), [Italia.it: local festivals and villages](https://www.italia.it/it/italia/cosa-fare/via-francigena-borghi-fioriti), [Italia.it: holidays](https://www.italia.it/it/italia/informazioni/orari-e-festivita-in-italia).

## Editorial limits and targets

Humor targets objects, the narrator, jargon and fictional procedures. No national inferiority/superiority, regional contempt, accent mockery, mafia caricature, graphic injury or sexual material. Dark and edgy material stays mild. Child-safe stories do not promote alcohol or unsafe festival participation.

Each literal joke needs a concrete Italian reference or an actual Italian linguistic mechanism. Metadata assembly is permitted; rotating words inside a joke template is not. Automated schema/duplicate checks do not establish comedic quality. Native editorial review of naturalness, regional precision and repetition remains outstanding.
